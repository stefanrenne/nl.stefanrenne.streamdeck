Control your Homey smart home directly from your Stream Deck — instantly, over your local network, without any cloud dependency.

Each LCD button on your Stream Deck becomes a powerful, customisable smart home trigger. Press a button and Homey runs your flows: turn lights on or off, adjust scenes, lock doors, or trigger any automation you've built.

---

Features:

Dashboards
Organise your buttons into multiple named dashboards and switch between them from within a Homey Flow. Build a "Morning" dashboard, a "Movie Night" dashboard, or any layout that fits your routine — then let Homey switch them automatically based on time, presence, or any other trigger.

Custom Images
Upload your own JPG or PNG images and assign them to buttons. Give every button a clear, personalised icon so you always know exactly what it does at a glance.

Dynamic Variables
Create variable buttons that display two lines of live text — temperature, a device state, a countdown, or any value your flows produce. Update the text in real time using Flow actions, so your Stream Deck always reflects what's happening in your home.

Flow Integration
- Triggers: React to button presses (down, up, single press, double press) in any Homey Flow. Each trigger provides the button's dashboard, image name, text lines, payload, column, and row as tokens.
- Conditions: Branch your flows based on which dashboard is currently active.
- Actions: Switch to any dashboard or update a variable button's text — first line, second line, or both.

Auto-Discovery
The app automatically detects your Stream Deck Network Dock on the local network via mDNS, so setup takes seconds.

---

Configuration:

Open the app settings in Homey to manage your dashboards, images, and variables. Use the Stream Deck Studio software to assign payloads and map each button to your Homey setup.

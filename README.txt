Control your Homey smart home directly from your Stream Deck — instantly, over your local network, without any cloud dependency.

Each LCD button on your Stream Deck becomes a powerful, customisable smart home trigger. Press a button and Homey runs your flows: turn lights on or off, adjust scenes, lock doors, or trigger any automation you've built.

Group your buttons into dashboards, give them your own images or live text, and let Homey switch dashboards automatically. All you need is a Stream Deck connected through a Stream Deck Network Dock.

Full documentation, including every Flow card and the supported Stream Deck models: https://github.com/stefanrenne/nl.stefanrenne.streamdeck
